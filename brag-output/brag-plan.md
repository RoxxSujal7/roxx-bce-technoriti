# Brag Plan: ROXX TECHNORITI '26

## What is this app?
ROXX TECHNORITI is the flagship web application for TECHNORITI '26 at Bihar College of Engineering, Bhagalpur (BCE Bhagalpur), delivering a high-performance interactive technical festival experience across 29 competition arenas, accommodation logistics, and real-time motion systems.

## The angle
College tech fest sites are usually broken, bloated, or non-functional mockups. ROXX TECHNORITI is the complete opposite: an obsessively crafted, production-grade web experience featuring continuous physics-based tickers, 3D elliptical card rings, interactive halftone canvas typography, and verified accommodation tariffs.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dramatic dark screen with subtle ambient glow cuts sharply to bold glowing magenta-purple typography: "TECHNORITI '26" with an active countdown/status beam and the institutional mark of BCE Bhagalpur.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Continuous 3-Row Ticker:** Restored frame-rate independent topic slider flowing multi-directionally at $300\text{px/s}$.
- **3D Halo Reel Showcase:** Elliptical 3D card ring rotating smoothly through featured arenas with synchronized title readouts.
- **29 Competition Arenas:** Cinematic list filtering 29 technical challenges across Robotics, Coding, Design, Gaming, and Quizzes with zero data truncation.
- **Accommodation Tariffs:** Verified 3-tier pricing (₹50 Lodging, ₹125 Mess, ₹175 Complete Stay) with rotating conic-gradient glow.
- **Interactive Halftone Canvas Footer:** Particle matrix repelling under cursor interaction across full $100\text{vw}$.

## Outro / punchline
"ROXX TECHNORITI '26 — Bihar College of Engineering, Bhagalpur. Engineered by Sujal Kumar."

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry on Homepage Hero with moving 3-tier slider.
2. Drag-interaction on 3D Halo Reel exploring featured arenas.
3. Transition to Events Page with live category filtering of all 29 arenas.
4. Accommodation tariff view with recommended complete stay package.
5. Final brand signature.
