## 7. Verified Festival FAQs

1. **How do I register for accommodation?**
   _You can book your stay by visiting the Register section on this portal. Check the 'Need Campus Accommodation' option and submit your details._
2. **Can we book as a team or group?**
   _Yes, teams and college contingents can book together. Groups with over 4 participants receive group tariff accommodations._
3. **What documents are mandatory for room allotment?**
   _1) College ID Card (2 photocopies + original), 2) Aadhaar / PAN or Government ID (2 copies), 3) 3 passport size photos, 4) College NOC, and 5) Copy of payment slip._
4. **Does the accommodation fee include food?**
   _Lodging is ₹50 per person per day. Mess dining (hygienic breakfast, lunch, and dinner) is ₹125 per day served in the college dining hall._
5. **Will team members be given accommodation together?**
   _Yes, participants from the same team or institution of the same gender are allotted shared rooms together based on availability._
6. **What are the check-in and check-out timings?**
   _Check-in desk opens at 6:00 AM on festival opening day at the BCE Bhagalpur Main Gate Reception. Standard check-out is by 10:00 PM on closing day._
7. **How can I cancel or change my booking?**
   _Contact the Boys or Girls Hostel Logistics Coordinator with your registered Mobile Number and College Roll Number before the final deadline._
